One-time & variable charges
Setup Fee (one-time)
PKR 15,000–30,000. Covers data migration from old system, training, and initial configuration.
Extra Students
PKR 15–25 per student per month beyond plan limit. Auto-billed, no manual upgrade needed.
Extra Campus
PKR 3,000–6,000 per additional campus per month. Applies from Growth plan onwards.
SMS / WhatsApp
Pass-through rate + 10–15% margin. Charged per message bundle, NOT per student.
Annual Discount
Pay 10 months upfront, get 2 months free. Improves your cash flow & reduces churn.
Free Trial
30 days, no card required. Full Starter plan, 1 school, 50 students cap. Auto-expires.
Pricing strategy: Founder's price — first 10–20 schools get their tier locked for life. Referral reward — bring another school, get 1 month free. These two hooks build early traction and word-of-mouth without discounting the published price.
