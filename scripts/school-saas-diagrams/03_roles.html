School SaaS — User Roles & Permissions Hierarchy

Top-down access control. Higher roles can do everything lower roles can — but every permission is also enforced server-side.

Super Admin (You)
Platform owner. Full control of every school & every plan.
• Create / suspend / delete any school
• Set pricing plans & limits
• View platform-wide reports & MRR
• Manage audit logs
owns & manages
School Admin
Owner of one school's data & settings.
• Setup classes, sections, subjects
• Invite & manage staff & users
• Configure fee plans
• View all school reports
manages staff of
Principal
Academic head.
• View & approve results
• Monitor attendance
• Manage teacher workload
• View school reports
Accountant
Handles money.
• Create invoices
• Record payments & receipts
• Track defaulters
• Generate financial reports
Admission Officer
CRM user.
• Log new inquiries
• Schedule follow-ups
• Convert lead → student
• Send offer messages
supervises
Teacher
Daily classroom operations.
• Mark own class attendance
• Enter exam marks
• View own class students
• Comment on student progress
serves
Parent
Read-only portal.
• View child's attendance
• Pay fees online
• View results & report cards
• Receive SMS / WhatsApp alerts
Student
Optional, read-only.
• View own attendance
• View own results
• View timetable
• (No editing rights)
Permission enforcement rule: The UI hides buttons the user cannot use, but the real check happens on the server. Every API call verifies the user's role AND their school_id. A teacher logged into School A can never mark attendance for School B, even by tampering with the request.
