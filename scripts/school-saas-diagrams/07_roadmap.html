Phase 2 — Academics + CRM
~6–8 weeks · after MVP
Outcome: Exams, result cards, SMS to parents, admission CRM. Billing: bump Starter → Growth upsell.
Phase 3 — Scale Modules
~8–10 weeks · after Phase 2
Outcome: Payroll, biometric, canteen POS, online payments, mobile app. Billing: Pro & Enterprise tier unlocks.
