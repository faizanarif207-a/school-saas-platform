Principal · Academic Head — key sections explained
1Sidebar
Academics-focused menu. Sees exams, results, teachers. Cannot edit fee plans or payroll.
2KPI cards
Academic health metrics: attendance, avg result, pending approvals queue.
3Approvals queue
Result cards submitted by teachers need principal sign-off before going to parents.
4Class attendance
Today's attendance per class. Auto-flags classes below 80% for 3+ days.
5Teacher performance
Cross-tab of result % + attendance-marking discipline. Identifies who needs coaching.
