02 · Logo

Primary logo

Teen rangeen bars ek mark banatay hain, jo mil kar pehle ek jhanda aur phir ek stack ka tasavvur deti hain. Wordmark "Emerson" dark grey mein hai.

Mark (icon) akela

Chhoti jagah ke liye (favicon, app icon, social avatar). Teen bars ko dobara arrange na karein.

Clear space aur minimum size

Logo ke chaaron taraf kam az kam ek bar ki height jitni khaali jagah rakhein. Digital par minimum width 120px, print par 30mm.

Clear space = height of one bar

Logo ke saath kya karein / na karein

DOSirf saaf (solid) background par use karein: white ya dark ink (#1C232D).
DOLogo ko proportion mein scale karein.
DON'TStretch ya squash na karein.
DON'TBars ke rang change na karein ya naye rang na dalein.
DON'TBars ko ghuma kar ya tilt karke na dikhayein.
DON'TWordmark par shadow, outline ya gradient na lagayein.

Note: Ye SVG recreation hai. Final production ke liye Emerson ki original vector file (SVG/AI) use karein.

06 · Voice & Tone

Kaisa bolna hai

Emerson direct, confident aur shafaf bolta hai. Bada-bada wada nahin, lekin numbers aur process ke saath sach.

Hum ye hain

  • Confident, lekin mughrur nahin
  • Simple words, technical jargon kam
  • Action-oriented (Start, Explore, Book)
  • Client ko partner samajhna

Hum ye nahin hain

  • Khokhla hype ("world's best" jaisi baatein bina saboot)
  • Bhaari corporate language
  • Darane wale sales lines
  • Emoji ka zyada istemal (sirf chhota mauqa par)
