Wireframe Guide
1Sidebar
Role-based navigation. Only Super Admin sees these menu items. Schools see their own scoped menu.
2Topbar
Page title + breadcrumb. The '+ Add School' button opens the new school form (Phase 1 task).
3KPI cards
At-a-glance platform health: total schools, active, on trial, and estimated monthly revenue (MRR).
4Schools table
All tenants in one view. Plan, student count vs limit, status pill. Click 'Manage' to drill into one school.
5Plan distribution
Visual breakdown of how many schools are on each tier. Helps you spot which plan is most popular & adjust pricing.
